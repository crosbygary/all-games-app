Support

Questions, feedback, or a game that isn’t working? Contact the All Games team.

Email support

Or write to cr0sby_gar@icloud.com.

What to include

  • The game name and what happened.
  • Your iPhone model, iOS version, and All Games app version.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue and, if useful, a screenshot.

Please don’t send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

A game or the catalog won’t load

  1. Check your internet connection. Games and the catalog need internet access.
  2. Close the game and open it again.
  3. Close and reopen All Games, then check for an app update.
  4. If the issue continues, email us with the game name.

Where are my favorites?

Tap the heart on a game card, then open Favorites. Favorites are saved on your device; the app does not provide account-based syncing between devices.

Do I need an account or subscription?

The current app does not require sign-in and has no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Privacy requests

For questions about your data or a privacy request, email cr0sby_gar@icloud.com with “All Games Privacy” in the subject. See our privacy policy for details.
