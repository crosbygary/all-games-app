Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This policy describes information handling in All Games for iPhone and on this support website. For privacy questions, contact the All Games team at cr0sby_gar@icloud.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your favorites, profile display name, preferences, and onboarding status locally. It also caches catalog information and artwork. The display name is not an online account, and the app does not send your local display name or favorites to its catalog service.

Catalog access and network requests

To access the catalog, the app creates a random device identifier and sends it to its catalog service. This is an app-generated identifier, not an advertising identifier. The identifier and authentication credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.

Catalog requests include information needed to deliver content, such as the app build, iOS platform, language/locale, and requested content or filters. The services receiving requests also receive your IP address and standard connection information. This information supports content delivery, authentication, and service operation.

Games and external content

Games run as web content within the app. Game providers and artwork hosts receive network requests when their content loads, including your IP address and browser or device information. Web games may use cookies, local storage, or other web technologies for their functionality, progress, analytics, or advertising, depending on the provider.

Those providers’ privacy practices and retention periods may differ from ours. Consult notices supplied by the game provider, or contact us with the game name if you need help identifying the provider. This policy does not promise that external game content is free of advertising or tracking.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and address the issue. Email is processed through the email services used to send and receive your message.

This website

This website does not add analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, or a contact form. Contact links open your email app. The site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub processes connection information when serving the website under its privacy statement.

Sharing and service providers

Information is processed by services involved in delivering the catalog, game content, website hosting, and email support. Data may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. External links and games are subject to the relevant provider’s policies.

Storage and your choices

You can remove individual favorites with the heart button or use Settings → Clear favorites. You can change your display name in Profile. Removing the app removes ordinary app-local data, but iOS Keychain identifiers can persist after uninstallation. External game providers control their own server-side records.

Contact us to ask about server-side retention or to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with you. Please describe your request without sending passwords or authentication tokens. We may need additional information to identify the relevant records. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, request portability, or complain to a data-protection authority.

Security and international processing

The app uses HTTPS for catalog access and stores authentication credentials in the iOS Keychain. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Hosting, game, and email providers may process information in countries other than your own.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require users to submit their age or create an account. Parents or guardians with questions about a child’s information should contact us. External games may have their own age requirements and privacy notices.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies this version. For questions or privacy requests, contact cr0sby_gar@icloud.com.
